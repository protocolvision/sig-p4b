**Subject:** Monday: what AI agents see when they look at your business

Hi all,

Thanks to everyone who came to the kickoff. 17 of us, almost two hours, and a great first chat. Three things that stuck with me:

- **You can't sue a swarm.** Agency law expects someone you can find and hold to account. An agent can be copied a thousand times and switched off in a second, so who do you go after?
- **"Autonomous" is often a legal costume.** Crypto has seen this a lot: call something decentralized and the liability goes away. DAO delegates drifting away from token holders look a lot like agents drifting away from us.
- **The paper is already out of date.** It's from early 2025, and much of what it predicted is now everyday news. The field moves faster than the rules do.
