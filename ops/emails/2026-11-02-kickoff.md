**Subject:** Monday 2 November: what keeps a business coherent when agents do the work?

Hi everyone,

AI agents don't wait for approvals, and no approval queue can keep up with them. Our bet for 2027 is that a business is held together by a small set of hard rules, such as who can sign a contract or move money, with everything around them left free. We'll spend the year finding out if that's right. Our year in review explains why: https://protocolized.summerofprotocols.com/p/protocols-for-business-2026-review
