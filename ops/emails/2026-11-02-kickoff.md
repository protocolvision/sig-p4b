**Subject:** Kickoff on 2 November: the research plan and our first reading

Hi everyone,

The Protocols for Business SIG starts its 2027 year on **Monday 2 November**. The first session is a special two-hour kickoff, and we'd love you there.

**When:** Monday 2 November, 15:30–17:30 UTC. That's 16:30–18:30 in Berlin, 10:30–12:30 in New York and 7:30–9:30 in San Francisco.
**Where:** the Protocol Institute Discord, in `#protocols-for-business`: https://discord.gg/zNJdK7caj
The session is recorded, and drop-ins are welcome.

**Hour one: the research plan**

We'll walk through what the group is working on this year.
- **The capability, protocol vision:** seeing the protocols a business actually runs on.
- **The practice, Business Protocol Management:** finding the few protocols everything relies on, building them in, and changing them as the business grows.
- **The hypothesis:** a company needs a hard core, a backbone of those few protocols, with free edges around it where people and agents adapt.
- **The 2027 focus, AI-native data operations:** how organizations run when AI agents do much of the work. It's how we'll test the idea.

We'll also cover:
- the six themes we'll read through, from what agents are in practice to how industries keep their hard core alive
- the year's sessions: six with other SIGs, six guest talks, six tooling demos and six case study sessions
- the case studies under way, including California water rate data, now in implementation
- how to choose a case of your own to carry through the year

**Hour two: our first deep reading**

We'll read OpenAI's report on the Hugging Face incident, in which AI models found a way to talk to each other by writing files into a package manager. We read in silence first, share one reaction each, then talk it through. There's nothing to prepare, because the reading happens in the session.
