Protocol watching

Notice one protocol at work, as a customer, or in public records: an approval chain, a form everyone fills in the same way, a spreadsheet that quietly runs a department. Log it with the Protocol Bicorder and post it in #protocols-for-business during the session. Over the year the group builds an inventory of about a hundred.
