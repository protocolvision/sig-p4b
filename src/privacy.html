Privacy

The site collects as little as it can: an email address if you ask for session emails, and what you choose to send through its forms.

  • Session emails

    To get session emails you give an email address. You can also add a name, website, GitHub and Discord handles, and an affiliation. They are kept by the group’s sign-up service on Cloudflare and used only to send the readings and notes before sessions. They are not sold or shared.

  • Leaving

    Every email has a way out, and the unsubscribe page removes your address straight away.

  • Discord notes

    When someone new signs up, the group’s Discord channel gets a short note with the new total and no personal details. The “Offer a talk” and “Request advisory services” forms post what you write, including how to reach you, to that channel so the facilitators can reply. Nothing from those forms is stored on our side.

  • Kickoff slides

    The kickoff slides record reactions, taps, poll answers and questions anonymously: your browser keeps a random ID so a tap counts once, and nothing else identifies you. Questions are shown to everyone without a name. If you introduce yourself or say you’re interested in paid work, your name, website, case study idea and email are stored on Cloudflare for the hosts only, never shown on the slides, and used only to follow up with you. Adding your blyg, blog or Substack posts your request to the group’s Discord channel, as on the Feed.

  • Browsing

    The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads; the kickoff slides keep only their random ID in your browser. Cloudflare, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs. To stop spam, the sign-up service counts requests from each network address for one hour, then forgets them.

  • Sessions

    Sessions are recorded, and recordings go in the archive.
