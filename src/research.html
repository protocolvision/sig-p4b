Research

Our 2027 focus is AI Native Data Operations: how organizations coordinate when much of the work is done by people working through AI and by agents talking to agents.

  • Emission

    What an organization emits, where, who can read it, and how long it stays current.

  • Evals & model ops

    Scoring what agents produce against real outcomes, and versioning models, prompts, and context.

  • AI-mediated work

    Delegating discretion to agents explicitly, checking it cheaply, and recording it where others can see.

Questions

  • What is an AI-native, post-platform organization?
  • What should an organization emit, to whom, and how quickly should it decay?
  • When people work as centaurs and agents talk to agents, where does discretion sit, and who checks it?
