What we do

  • Sessions

    Every other Monday we meet to read one primary source closely, together. The reading plan grows with what participants bring.

  • Research

    In 2027 we study AI-native data operations, meaning how organizations run when AI agents do much of the work. Members are encouraged to complete a case study during the year.

  • Training

    We train protocol vision, the capability to see the protocols a business runs on, through protocol watching, workshops and simulations.
