Protocols for Business

A research group of the Protocol Institute. We study how organizations coordinate through protocols, and what changes as AI agents join the work. Over the year, members build protocol vision: the capability to see the rules a business really runs on, and to help decide which should be hard. We also practise Business Protocol Management: seeing, designing and evolving the few hard protocols that define a business and guide its people and agents.

Joining details

What you’ll learn to do

  • Prepare

    Explore how our roles and professional identity change as AI comes to mediate business work, in the sessions and on the blyg.

  • See

    Spot the protocols a business runs on, written and unwritten, by watching for one protocol every two weeks, like birdwatching, with a short comment for the group.

  • Read

    Read incidents and agent traces for where rules held, broke or were missing, in the sessions.

  • Design and test

    Decide what to harden and what to keep soft, and try it in a case study or the simulation.

  • Evolve

    Revise a hard protocol when the business or its agents outgrow it, following the Business Protocol Management guide.

  • Write it up

    Describe a messy system so the people inside it recognise it, on the blyg or in a case brief.

These are the skills in our job description for a Business Protocol Lead.

How we will learn

  • Sessions

    Every other Monday we meet to read one primary source closely, together. The reading plan grows with what participants bring.

  • Research

    In 2027 we study AI-native data operations, meaning how organizations run when AI agents do much of the work. Members are encouraged to complete a case study during the year.

  • Training

    We train protocol vision, the capability to see the protocols a business runs on, through protocol watching, workshops and simulations. Each member logs one protocol every two weeks.
