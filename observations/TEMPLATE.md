# Name the protocol in a few words

**Where:** industry and type of organization. Don't name private organizations or people.

**What it coordinates:** who or what it lines up, and what moves through it (documents, approvals, money, attention, data).

**Form:** document · signature · meeting · form · software rule · metric · trace or signal · habit

**Hard or soft:** is it enforced by code, contract, or law, or held together by norms and habit?

**Who can change it:** and how often that actually happens.

**What AI is doing to it:** what has changed already, or what you expect to change.

**Reading it speaks to:** which syllabus text or claim it supports, complicates, or contradicts.
