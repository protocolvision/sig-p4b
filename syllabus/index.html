Protocols for Business SIG Syllabus Case studies

Syllabus: October 2026 – March 2027

Twelve sessions, every other Monday. Dates are proposed and will be confirmed on the calendar.

The syllabus has one goal: to get the AI Native Data Operations project to its 2027 charter with evidence it can defend. We start with the model, test it against two real engagements, and then write down what held up so that someone else can use it in another domain.

Each month ends with a deliverable, a concrete artifact that goes into the project repo. Readings marked core should be read before the session. Readings marked background are optional.

Shared case-study template

Both cases, and every future engagement, answer the same questions in the same order. That makes them comparable and lets them serve as evidence for the model.

  1. Context: the industry, the organization's role, and what the data is for.
  2. Bottleneck: where the work was actually stuck, and what everyone assumed was the problem.
  3. Protocols before: how data was found, extracted, checked, and trusted.
  4. Intervention: what became code, what was left to agents, and what stayed with people.
  5. Evidence: what was measured and against what, including where the model was wrong.
  6. Maturity mapping: the level before and after, and which move made the difference.
  7. What transfers: the parts of the playbook another domain could reuse.
