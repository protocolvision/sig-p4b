# A drawing-sheet kit for the year

::: generated
Every figure on this site is drawn the same way: hairlines on paper, centre lines, dimension ticks, numbered callouts, hatching for anything solid, and a small title block. It is the language of architectural and engineering drawings, and it fits the subject. A drawing set is itself a protocol: a few strict conventions that let many trades build one thing without meeting.

We've collected those drawings, and a set of banners, call backgrounds and post images made in the same style, into one kit: **[protocolvision.org/kit](https://protocolvision.org/kit/)**. Everything there is free for members and friends of the group to use.

![The hard core drawing from theme V drafting itself: a hatched core rising through free floor plates, line by line.](media/hardcore-drafting.svg)

## The four concepts

The banners and posts are built on four drawings, each one an idea from the practice guide.

- **Protocol vision.** A network of protocols, mostly in fog, with patches of focus drifting across it. Inside the focus the links sharpen, the hard ones show in colour, and the protocols get names: approval, handoff, permission, spend limit. Seeing the protocols a business runs on is the first step.
- **Hard core.** The network zooms into its centre, which resolves into eight protocols most companies seem to share: signing authority, spending limits, payment approval, access rights, change control, data out, output checks and an audit trail. The list is a proposal the group is testing.
- **Core plan.** An architect's floor plan: a grid of columns with the structural core hatched in the middle and a dimension line marked *keep small*. In a building, the core is the part that holds the rest up. It is also the mark of the new advisory page.
- **Interface.** One hard line with branches growing from it that nobody planned. A protocol does its job by widening what others can do.

## The figures from this site

The kit also holds the drawings from the Research, Sessions and About pages, now with animated versions that draft themselves line by line:

- **The six themes:** Braitenberg vehicles around a light (agents), a murmuration of starlings with one bird's seven neighbours (nature), signals spreading in rings (emissions), a crack across a grid of blocks (incidents), a hard core rising through floor plates (hardness) and a heartbeat trace (liveness).
- **The practice:** an append-only field log, bacteria switching on together past a quorum threshold, lines converging on a single point, and the hard core in axonometric.

## Formats

Each concept comes as a LinkedIn banner, a video-call background, a post image, a square post, a story and an X or Bluesky header, in light and dark mode, with a forest or cobalt accent. The animated ones also come as MP4 and GIF. Call backgrounds keep the drawing to one side, so it doesn't sit behind your head; LinkedIn banners keep it to the right, clear of your profile photo.

Click any image on the kit page to see it full screen and zoom in.

## For assistants

Every asset has a written description on the page, and [index.json](https://protocolvision.org/kit/index.json) lists each one with its address, size and format. An assistant asked to "find a drawing of a hard core for my slides" can read the list and pick one.
