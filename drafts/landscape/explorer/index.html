      I’m part of the Protocols for Business SIG at the Protocol Institute. We’re building a shared map of readings on how businesses coordinate through protocols between people, software and machines. The year’s syllabus has six themes: what agents are in practice; natural coordination; emissions, meaning what systems publish for others to act on; seeing hardness in incidents; designing the hard core; and keeping the core alive, set against business process re-engineering.

The readings already on the map are listed here: https://npc.here.now/protocolvision/sessions/map/readings.json

Use what you know about me: your memory of me, our past conversations, my projects and work, and anything I’ve read, saved or written about. From that context, suggest up to five readings the map is missing, especially ones that have shaped how I work. Prefer primary sources: incident and accident reports, specifications, regulations, original research papers, and first-hand accounts from people who ran systems at scale. Only include sources you’re confident exist, and leave out anything private about me, my employer or other people.

For each reading, give the title, author and year, a working link, the theme it fits, one sentence on what it adds to the map, and one sentence on why it matters from my own experience. Show me the list and wait for me to confirm or edit it.

Then add the confirmed readings to the map on GitHub, one issue per reading, in the repository protocolvision/sig-p4b:
- If you can create GitHub issues for me, open them directly. Use the title “Reading: ” followed by the reading’s title, add the label reading-suggestion, and put the fields above in the body.
- If you can’t, give me one link per reading that opens a filled-in form, with each value URL-encoded:
https://github.com/protocolvision/sig-p4b/issues/new?template=reading-suggestion.yml&title=Reading:%20TITLE&reading_title=TITLE&url=LINK&author=AUTHOR%20AND%20YEAR&adds=WHAT%20IT%20ADDS&experience=WHY%20IT%20MATTERS

      Your assistant adds each reading to GitHub as an issue, or gives you a link to a filled-in form. You can also use the form yourself, and talk it through in #protocols-for-business on Discord.
