# Functional analysis: design (descriptive, added at the user's request)

Written 11 October 2026 before any computation. Descriptive only: no hypothesis tests, no pre-registered thresholds. Nothing here changes a rerun finding.

## Function map
One map, defined once in `function_map.py`. Fifteen functions: sales, customer service/support, marketing, finance and accounting, HR, legal and compliance, IT and infrastructure, security, software engineering, data and AI/ML, operations and supply chain, manufacturing/field, healthcare/clinical, executive/strategy, other. Order of assignment: title keywords for security, legal, support, data/AI, sales (plus a short software-engineering keyword list), then the O*NET detailed code of `postings-mapped-v2.csv` (major group and minor group, with detailed codes where a minor group straddles functions). Title-to-major-group accuracy is about 83% (deviation 18), so every function figure carries that limit. A 60-posting hand spot check is reported.

## Questions and measures
- Q1 Demand for AI capability. Per function: share of the function's postings whose title carries an AI/ML/automation/agentic term (the deviation 14 term list; "agent (human)" titles excluded), weighted by the rest20 sampling weight `sw`; Wilson interval using the Kish effective sample size. To avoid circularity (the title term would otherwise define "data and AI/ML"), function for Q1 is assigned with the AI terms stripped from the title. Cross-check: a census of all titles in `postings-mapped-v2.csv`.
- Q2 Agent duties in existing jobs. Existing-title postings only (S1 + rest20). By function: (a) weighted share with at least one lexically flagged task, Wilson on Kish n; (b) two-phase estimate of postings with at least one confirmed agent duty (AO, PE or RD), using the group confirmation rates of `i1_twophase.py` and its 1 - (1 - p)^k rule, as a share of the function's weighted existing postings; the band is the Wilson interval of the group confirmation rates carried through (it does not include sampling or lexical-recall error); (c) direct confirmation rate of phase-2 sampled tasks by function (Wilson). The estimate is checked against the totals in `I1-twophase.md`.
- Q3 Which activities sit where. Final phase-2 codes (agreed, else adjudicated) joined to posting function: counts of AO, PE, PV, RD (a task with two codes counts under each) by function, with the share of sampled tasks in the function. Counts are of a stratified task sample (150 S1, 300 rest20, 150 new-title), unweighted, so they are not rates in the population. Platform-controls P2 posting items (tool_change, decision) by function.
- Q4 Accountability and reporting lines. AI-titled (new-title, non-human-agent) postings; text from the raw postings, joined by doc_id = "P" + ats + "_" + board_id + "_" + id. A regex extracts "reports to / reporting to / reports directly to" phrases and "part of the ... team" phrases; each is classified to a department by keyword rules and tabulated, with the posting's own function crossed against it. Verbatim examples of at most 40 words with posting ids, with emails, phone numbers, URLs and personal names removed. Phrases themselves are not stored in tables.
- Q5 Security and service control. Duty statements in `postings-haiku-all.tasks.jsonl` joined to posting function. Four keyword families (listed in `q5_functional.py` and in results.md): security review, AI governance, guardrail/permission, service management. Rate per 1,000 duty statements by function, weighted by the posting's `sw`, Wilson on Kish n of duty statements. Also the share of postings in the function with at least one such duty.
- Speech cross-check. I6 function counts of AO and PE spans from `I6-tables.csv`, reported as pooled spans (per-function totals reflect transcript counts, not rates; extraction recall about 0.5).

## Limits stated up front
Function from title is about 83% right at major-group level; the sample is employer-limited (Fortune 500 and VC software boards); all intervals are conditional on the function map; phase-2 tasks are a sample of lexically flagged tasks only.

## Changes made after seeing data (recorded 11 October 2026)
- The title-keyword map gained short finance, HR, marketing, software and executive lists, and was revised once after a first 60-posting spot check (73% correct) exposed rule errors; a fresh 60-posting draw scores the final map. Both results are reported.
- Q2 interval: percentile bootstrap over postings and the phase-2 sample replaces the design's rate-band as the main interval (the band is kept in tables.csv as `confirmed_agent_duty_band_rates_only`).
- Q5 interval: percentile bootstrap over postings within sampling group replaces Wilson on Kish n, because duty statements cluster in postings. Posting-share rows keep Wilson on Kish n.
- Q5 keyword lists were revised once after reading the most frequent matches (dropped bare "authorization", "escalation", "root cause analysis", "change management", "risk assessment"; moved "incident response" and "AI security" to security review).
- Q4 phrase extraction was revised twice for abbreviation periods, verb uses of "report to" and clause cutting.
