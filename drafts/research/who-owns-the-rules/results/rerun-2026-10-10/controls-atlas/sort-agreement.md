# Card sort agreement

On the 662-control sample:
- Object axis, sort S vs sort H: ARI 0.59, NMI 0.70
- Mechanism axis, sort S vs sort H: ARI 0.45, NMI 0.59
- Embedding clusters (non-noise, n=459) vs object S: ARI 0.38, NMI 0.59; vs object H: ARI 0.29, NMI 0.55

Embedding clusters are finer-grained (36) than the sorts (13–14), so NMI is the more informative measure there.
