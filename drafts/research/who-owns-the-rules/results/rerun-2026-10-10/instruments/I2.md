# I2: task clusters from today's postings, and coding of cluster packets

## Method
- Task statements from 14,079 postings were embedded (all-mpnet-base-v2), reduced (UMAP) and clustered (HDBSCAN, min cluster size 30) on a discovery half; a holdout half was projected onto the clusters. A discovery cluster is "found" when predicted holdout size is at least 30, best Jaccard at least 0.5, overall ARI at least 0.6 and employers at least 10. Labels and function families were assigned per cluster (`clusters/leaf/labels.csv`; 8 labels marked reviewed among the 11 found).
- Coding: two coders applied the codes PV, PE, RD, AO, OT (definitions in the codebook, `loop-design-v2.md` section 6) to 410 records and to 8 packets (one per work cluster); seeded cases scored recall and precision (`coding/scores.md`). A reviewer coded 40 real records blind to the coders (`review/coding-real-check.md`).
- Numbers below are copied from those files; long-format data in `I2-tables.csv`.

## Results

### Clustering
- Pool: 213,938 tasks after performer filter and within-employer dedupe; 50,675 after a cap of 300 per employer. Discovery 25,122 tasks, holdout 25,553. Noise fraction 0.648 (discovery) and 0.635 (holdout). 8,832 discovery tasks sit in the 114 clusters.
- Stability: holdout ARI 0.8269 (gate 0.6, n=3,861 non-noise in both); mean pairwise ARI across three UMAP seeds 0.9639.
- 114 discovery clusters; 11 found, 103 not found. Median discovery size of not-found clusters 54 tasks (range 30-247).

### Reviewer check on 40 real records
- The reviewer coded 3 of 40 records as containing PV, PE or RD work (R0054, R0342, R0397); 35 as OT or AO only.
- Exact code-set match: reviewer vs A 36 of 40; reviewer vs B 37 of 40; A vs B 35 of 40. Any rule-work present/absent: 39 of 40 (reviewer vs A), 39 of 40 (reviewer vs B), 38 of 40 (A vs B).
- Coder A coded 2 of the reviewer's 3 rule-work records (R0054 coded OT); coder B coded all 3 (R0088 also coded PE by B, which the reviewer read as agent-building without a stated condition). The reviewer's note: with 3 positives, coder A's rule-work recall is 2/3, below the 0.7 bar in the codebook.
- Dated quote (reviewer file, rerun 2026-10-10), R0397: "turn goals into … rules, constraints … that AI systems can execute".

## Data gaps
- 103 of 114 clusters were not found, including the three AI-labelled clusters 110, 112 and 113; their absence reflects the holdout-stability test, not an absence of such statements in the postings.
- Only 8 labels were reviewed by a person among the found clusters; the others are model-assigned (`reviewed` column). Central spans in `clusters.csv` are the top 20 per cluster; `assignments.csv.gz` (task-to-cluster) was not read for this summary, so employers named per cluster are counts only.
- Packet coding rests on the 8 packets; shares are coder estimates. Real-record counts (PV 1 to 4, PE 2 to 6) are small, so any difference between coders is a difference of a handful of records. Coder B coded 21% of negative seeds as rule work.
- Task statements come from postings on the boards listed in the corpus (Workday, Greenhouse, Ashby, SmartRecruiters); cluster sizes depend on the 300-per-employer cap.
