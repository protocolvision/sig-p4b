# I1 labour-market measures: method and numbers

Script: `loop-tools/i1_labour.py` (run with the analytics venv). Specification: deviations 14, 18, 21. Tables: `I1-tables.csv` (measure, slice, value, n, weighting). Posting-level data: `I1-posting-level.csv.gz`. Cluster labels used: labels: reviewed version (`clusters/leaf/labels.csv` after the Opus review; cluster 19 = operations/mixed).

## Data

- 25,512 selected postings from 198 employers (selection.csv): 2,790 new-title, 584 new-title human-agent, 831 study-occupation (S1), 21,307 stratified 20% sample of the rest (rest20). 24,060 postings are Fortune 500 (166 employers), 1,452 are venture-backed software (32 employers).
- 305,533 Haiku tasks; 25,220 postings have at least one task.
- "New-title" means group `newtitle`; the human-agent group is reported separately and excluded from the absorption denominator.
- The rest20 group is a 20% sample and is not scaled up. Ratios using "all non-new-title postings" therefore count sampled postings.

## Lexical agent/AI duty flag

A task is flagged if its span matches, case-insensitively and as whole words, any of: AI, A.I., agentic, LLM, GenAI, generative AI, machine learning, automation, prompt, copilot, intelligent automation, RPA, AI governance, responsible AI, model risk; or "agent"/"agents" immediately adjacent (before or after, separated by space, hyphen or slash) to AI, A.I., agentic, autonomous, virtual or digital. No plurals or stems are added beyond the agent rule. A posting has an agent or AI duty if at least one of its tasks is flagged (all Haiku tasks, any performer).

Validation input: the 238 posting records of `corpus/b-raw/coding/record-pool.jsonl`. Records were identified as real when their span equals a Haiku task span: 200 real, 38 other. The flag is true for 21 of the 200 real records (the 7 flagged non-real records are not stored). Stored in `I1-lexical-flags.csv` (id, flag; 200 rows). The key was not opened; comparison with coder codes is not yet done.

## Cross-function postings

Function families and business/technical class come from the reviewed labels. A posting combines functions if its clustered tasks fall in two or more families, with at least one cluster labelled business and one labelled technical (clusters labelled "mixed" count as a family but as neither). Postings are included in the denominator only if at least one of their tasks was in the clustering pool (14,079 of 25,512 postings) and in a cluster of the set concerned. The pool held at most 300 tasks per employer, so a posting's tasks may be partly absent.

Primary set: clusters 2, 19, 43, 67, 69, 76, 83, 111.
Secondary set: all clusters except noise and the language artefacts. Artefact rule from deviation 20 recomputed from the 20 central spans: clusters 0, 1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 (8 of 114; 0, 6, 10 are the three found ones).

| Set | Posting group | Postings in set | Cross-function | Share | Share, employer-weighted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | new-title | 158 | 1 | 0.006 | 0.017 |
| Primary | other postings | 880 | 3 | 0.003 | 0.006 |
| Secondary | new-title | 886 | 73 | 0.082 | 0.151 |
| Secondary | other postings | 6,324 | 534 | 0.084 | 0.147 |

Other postings split: primary S1 1 of 67, rest20 2 of 803; secondary S1 38 of 310, rest20 486 of 5,892. Human-agent group: primary 0 of 10; secondary 10 of 122. Primary counts are very small.

## Major groups (descriptive only)

Occupation major groups come from the v2 model mapping, which has a measured major-group accuracy of about 83% (deviation 18). Every figure is descriptive and carries that error. Shares (CSV, `major_group_share`): new-title postings are 61.7% group 15, 15.1% group 11, 10.2% group 17; study occupations are 60.4% group 15, 22.1% group 13, 15.3% group 11; the rest sample is led by group 41 (20.2%), 15 (13.9%), 11 (12.4%). The share of new-title postings with a flagged duty per major group is also in the CSV. Detailed O*NET codes are not used.

## Limits

- The 20% rest sample is unscaled; the lexical flag includes broad terms (automation, prompt) by design.
- Cross-function measures rest on pooled tasks only, and non-English postings sit largely in artefact clusters or noise.
- Location and sector rules are described above; 2,257 postings have no US/non-US class.
