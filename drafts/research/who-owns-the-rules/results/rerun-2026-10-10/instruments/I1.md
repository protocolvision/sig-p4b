# I1 labour-market measures: method and numbers (v2)

Script: `loop-tools/i1_labour.py` (run with the analytics venv). Specification: deviations 14, 18, 21. Tables: `I1-tables.csv` (measure, slice, value, n, weighting, flag_precision). Posting-level data: `I1-posting-level.csv.gz`. Sampling strata: `I1-strata.csv`. Cluster labels used: the reviewed version (`clusters/leaf/labels.csv`; cluster 19 = operations/mixed).

v2 replaces v1 (`I1-v1.md`, `I1-tables-v1.csv`) after review (`review/I1-review.md`). Changes: rest20 sampling weights; the lexical flag validated against the coders; a corrected US/non-US rule; board workday/abbott attributed to Abbott Laboratories instead of Ross Stores.

## Data and weights

- 25,512 selected postings from 198 employers (selection.csv): 2,790 new-title, 584 new-title human-agent, 831 study-occupation (S1), 21,307 rest20. 24,060 postings are Fortune 500 (166 employers), 1,452 are venture-backed software (32 employers).
- 305,533 Haiku tasks; 25,220 postings have at least one task.
- **rest20 is a sample.** It is a 20% random sample of the 106,510 titled postings outside S1 and the new-title groups, stratified by O*NET major group (the selection-time mapping, `postings-mapped.csv`) × employer type, 39 strata. Each rest20 posting carries the weight N/n of its stratum (mean 5.00; range 1.0 to 6.0; the overall fraction is 0.200). S1 and both new-title groups are fully enumerated and carry weight 1. Weighted, the selection stands for all 110,715 titled postings.
- Three weightings are reported side by side:
  - **unweighted:** raw counts of selected postings (the v1 basis);
  - **sampling-weighted:** rest20 postings scaled to their stratum; these are estimates for the full crawl;
  - **employer-weighted:** each employer has total weight 1, spread over its postings in proportion to their sampling weights.
- Figures that use S1 or new-title postings alone do not change under sampling weights.

## Lexical agent/AI duty flag

**Definition (unchanged, deviation 21).** A task is flagged if its span matches, case-insensitively and as whole words, any of: AI, A.I., agentic, LLM, GenAI, generative AI, machine learning, automation, prompt, copilot, intelligent automation, RPA, AI governance, responsible AI, model risk. "Agent" or "agents" also counts when it sits directly before or after AI, A.I., agentic, autonomous, virtual or digital (joined by a space, hyphen or slash). No plurals or stems are added beyond the agent rule. A posting has an agent or AI duty if at least one of its tasks is flagged (all Haiku tasks, any performer).

**Validation.** The check uses the 200 real posting records of the coding pool. Real records were identified by the pool key, and each record's span equals its Haiku task span. A coder counts as calling a record an agent or AI duty if code1 or code2 is AO, PE or RD.

| Reference | Flag yes, coder yes | Flag yes, coder no | Flag no, coder yes | Flag no, coder no | Precision | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coder A | 1 | 20 | 3 | 176 | 0.05 (95% CI 0.01–0.23) | 0.25 (1 of 4) |
| Coder B | 3 | 18 | 4 | 175 | 0.14 (95% CI 0.05–0.35) | 0.43 (3 of 7) |

The coders agree with each other on 98.5% of the 200 records.

- **Precision is below 0.5 against both coders.** Of the 18 records flagged that neither coder called an agent or AI duty, 13 contain "AI" and 5 contain "automation". One each contains "machine learning", "generative AI" or "agentic". Most of them name AI as a topic of the work, such as securing AI services or evaluating AI technology, without an agent or AI duty in the coders' sense. Generic "automation" (test automation, plant automation) and "Machine Learning" as a team name make up the rest.
- **Recall is low.** The flag misses "agent infrastructure", "multi-agent systems" and "LLMs": the term list has no plural for LLM, and the agent rule needs one of the listed neighbours.
- **What the flag measures.** It measures mention of the listed terms in a task. It does not measure agent or AI duties as the coders code them. Precision is measured per task. A posting is flagged when any one of its tasks is flagged, so posting-level precision is unlikely to be higher.
- **Which terms drive the posting flag.** Among flagged existing postings (S1 + rest20, sampling-weighted 14,833), 4,231 are flagged only by "automation", 1,251 only by "prompt", and 4,329 only by "AI". In S1, 147 of the 400 flagged postings are flagged only by "automation" and/or "prompt". As a task word, "prompt" often means "quick" ("prompt and courteous service").
- **Matching detail.** Case-insensitive matching also picks up the Italian preposition "ai" (30 tasks). The term list is left as pre-registered.

The term list is not changed. Every figure below that uses the flag is reported with **flag precision 0.05 (coder A) / 0.14 (coder B)**. The CSV carries this in the `flag_precision` column. Per-record results are in `I1-lexical-flags.csv` (id, flag, matched terms, both coders' codes).

Type-by-location slices are in the CSV.

**US vs non-US rule.** The rule classifies the `location` text of postings-mapped-v2.csv by keywords, state codes and place names, using the first location when several are listed. The v2 rule corrects these v1 errors:
- a state-name list that matched "New", "North", "South", "West", "York", "Island" and "of" on their own;
- non-US place names that are also US places (Florence KY, North Wales PA, Vancouver WA, Melbourne FL, Dublin OH, New Mexico);
- site-code prefixes read as country codes ("CAN05:" for US sites);
- "U.S." at the end of a string;
- country-region prefixes ("DE-BY-…", "GB-AC-…");
- Canadian province codes.

Result: 15,080 US, 8,215 non-US and 2,217 unknown postings, against 14,974 / 8,281 / 2,257 under v1. Unknown postings are excluded from the US/non-US slices. Accuracy was checked by hand on two random samples of 50 postings:
- On the classified postings, v1 was 92 of 93 correct and v2 94 of 94. The one v1 miss is a store name classified US because it contains "North"; the text does not show where it is.
- Unknown postings: v1 3 and v2 1 in sample 1; v1 4 and v2 5 in sample 2. Three of the five v2 unknowns in sample 2 are US places that the rule does not list.

On all postings, v2 makes these changes:
- 175 non-US to US (80 of them "Florence, KY");
- 135 unknown to a class;
- 13 US to non-US (Canadian provinces, country-region prefixes);
- 95 US to unknown, where v1 had relied on "North", "West" or "of".

Every location string that moved into a class was read, and none of them was wrong. The rule is not the hand-checked procedure behind planned-4.3-located.csv.

## Cross-function postings

Function families and the business/technical class come from the reviewed labels. A posting combines functions if its clustered tasks fall in two or more families, with at least one cluster labelled business and one labelled technical. Clusters labelled "mixed" count as a family but as neither class. A posting enters the denominator only if at least one of its tasks was in the clustering pool and in a cluster of the set concerned (14,079 of 25,512 postings are in the pool). The pool held at most 300 tasks per employer, so a posting's tasks may be partly absent. That cap is a second sampling stage, which these weights do not correct.

- **Primary set:** the found clusters that are not language artefacts: 2, 19, 43, 67, 69, 76, 83, 111.
- **Secondary set:** all clusters except noise and the language artefacts.
- **Artefacts:** the deviation 20 rule, recomputed from the 20 central spans, marks clusters 0, 1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 (8 of 114; 0, 6 and 10 are the three found ones).

## Limits

- **The lexical flag does not identify agent or AI duties as the coders code them.** Its precision is 0.05 to 0.14 and its recall 0.25 to 0.43 (200 records, few positives). Absorption figures count postings that mention the listed terms. Read this way, the sampling-weighted (b) ratio of 5.3 says that existing postings that mention these terms outnumber new-title postings about five to one in the crawl.
- Sampling weights are inverse stratum fractions from the selection-time mapping. Clustering-pool caps are not corrected.
- Location and sector rules are described above. 2,217 postings have no US/non-US class.
- Employer counts with at least one flagged existing posting (95 for S1, 173 for all non-new-title) are lower bounds for (b), since rest20 is a sample.
