## Method
- 862 extracted spans from 34 of 38 transcripts (4 transcripts yielded no spans), coded by two coders with adjudication; final codes in `coding/speech-final.csv`. Codes: AO (agent operations, as labelled in coder notes), PE (setting what agents may do), PV (policy variance or inconsistency, coder notes only), OT (other / background / use). One span (M001_c02:6) carries AO+PE and is counted under each; no span has a final RD code.
- Extraction recall is about 0.5 (hand check 0.61; model-to-model agreement 0.51 to 0.60), precision 0.83, so every count below is a lower bound and absence of a span is not evidence of absence.
- Transcripts without timestamps (M041, M053, M054, M067) have no reliable timestamps; these are marked n/a. Some dates are approximate (M030 "2026", M067 "2025", M086 "about a year before Oct 2026", M084 "2026-09", M050 "2025-06").
