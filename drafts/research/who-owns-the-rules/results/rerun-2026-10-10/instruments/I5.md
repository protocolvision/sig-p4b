# I5: vendor role definitions, certifications, relabelling and the dated timeline

## Method
- Vendor documentation pages, release notes, certification pages and announcements were fetched on 2026-10-10 (search log `I5-search-log.csv`); for 12 vendors the role name, the duties sentence and the date type were recorded (`I5-vendor-roles.csv`, 30 rows). Where a page is dynamic or blocked, the cell says NF (not found) and the row is kept.
- Dates are stated with their type (definition published, release note, product GA, announcement, certification launch). A first archive capture (Wayback) is not a publication date; it is shown only as the earliest capture found.
- Rules naming the work are taken from I4 (effective or publication dates as printed). Practitioner-community events and open tooling were not collected (`I5-community-tooling.csv` is absent). Long-format data: `I5-tables.csv`.

## Results

### Counts
- Vendor roles: 30 rows across 12 vendors (Microsoft, Google, AWS, Workato, Workday, ServiceNow, Salesforce, Zendesk, Intercom, Okta, OpenAI, Anthropic); 23 rows are agent-specific and 7 are general workspace or account roles. Role types: admin 15, owner 6, other 6, sponsor 1, operator 1, not found 1. 14 rows have a dated event; 16 have no date (NF). 15 rows have an earliest archive capture.
- Certifications: 21 rows (17 vendor, 4 non-vendor); a launch, announcement or first-mention date was found for 9, and 5 of those are agent-specific (see timeline); 12 have no date (NF).
- Relabelling: 6 rows (Microsoft, Salesforce and Google renamed a product, role or certification to wording carrying agent or AI terms; Zendesk reused an existing role for AI agents).
- Browser pass: 19 items covering dynamic pages (OpenAI, Okta, Salesforce, ServiceNow) and date checks; 8 returned no quote or NF.

## Data gaps
- No data on practitioner-community events or first open tooling: `I5-community-tooling.csv` is absent, and no search in the log targeted them.
- Many pages are dynamic or blocked (Salesforce, ServiceNow, OpenAI HTTP 403 on initial fetch, Intercom, Okta): the role wording is NF in the archive; the browser pass found part of it. The Wayback archive returned no 2025 captures for Microsoft Entra Agent ID pages, and several lookups failed with connection errors on 2026-10-10, so first-seen dates in 2025 for those pages cannot be set from the archive. Raw archived HTML may lack client-rendered text, so "phrase not in archive" does not prove the wording was absent.
- Several certification launch dates come from search summaries or second-hand pages (IAPP date second-hand; AWS AIF-C01 June 2024 secondary; Salesforce AI Specialist original launch not found; GitHub GH-600 unverified).
- The `posting_wording_match` column is "NF (step not run)" for all roles: no comparison of vendor role wording with posting wording was made here.
- Vendors covered are 12; others (for example Oracle, SAP, IBM, UiPath role pages) are not in the file except UiPath certifications.
