# I3: period postings and today's AI or agent postings

## Method
- Period postings: the 82 single-page postings in `I3-period-postings.csv` (texts in `corpus/b-raw/history/`): history a, 60 operations and systems-engineering postings (2011-2016); history b, 17 trading systems and risk postings (2007-2015); history c, 5 grid control-room postings (2009-2014). Each was read for titles, the department or team named, stated purpose and background asked, and tagged with keyword-presence flags (page boilerplate lines removed first).
- Today: a random 30 (Python `random.seed(20261010)`) of the 2,132 postings in `corpus/b-raw/mapping/selection.csv` (group `newtitle`, 2,790 rows) whose title contains AI, agent, generative, LLM, copilot, prompt, machine-learning or ML wording. Texts from `corpus/b-raw/extract/postings-input.jsonl`. Team, function, duties, hire origin and AI-experience asked were coded by reading each text.
- Keyword flags are presence tests (a posting either contains a term or not), shown as counts of postings; they do not measure how central a duty is. Posting ids: `a-NN`, `b-NN`, `c-NN` are the history files in order; today's postings are numbered 1-30 below with full ids in `I3-tables.csv`. Long-format data: `I3-tables.csv`.

## Data gaps
- History a has nothing from 2008-2010 and 3 postings in 2014; it is drawn from two sites (jobs.github.com 2011-13 and Stack Overflow Careers 2015-16), so the change between 2011-13 and 2014-16 is partly a change of source. 
- History b has 17 postings (target 30-60), none for 2005-06 and one or fewer per year after 2010; at least 10 are recruiter-posted and 10 are 2007-08 efinancialcareers pages. History c has 5 (target 20-60), 3 from one grid operator in 2010, none for 2005-08 or 2011-13, and only c-03 is a system-operator role at a grid operator. See `I3-coverage.md` (archive access limits: do not read as absence).
- Keyword flags over-count in places (for example "control", "standards", "production environment") and the software-experience flag includes any programming-language name; the counts show whether a word is present, not whether the duty is central. Years figures above 15 were dropped as parse errors.
- Today's sample is 30 of 2,132 title matches; 6 of 30 come from one employer (Nvidia) and the pool is drawn from the Workday, Greenhouse, Ashby and SmartRecruiters boards in the corpus. Team, function and hire-origin codes are the author's reading of each text, single-coded, not double-coded.
- "Same function" in the hire-origin coding means the experience asked matches the function the work sits in as read from the text; it does not test where actual hires came from.
