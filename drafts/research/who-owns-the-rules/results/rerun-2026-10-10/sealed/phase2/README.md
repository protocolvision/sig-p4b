Built by `build.py` (scratchpad): sentence/table-row/CSV-row extraction of every claim with a number, date or quote (plus bolded or verdict-bearing characterisations) from the round-one loop, industry, roles and exploratory files; text cut to 40 words.
URLs come from the claim itself, its footnote or numbered reference, its record ids (pool/ledger), its timeline year, or its enclosing block (url_scope); they are matched to corpus ids and status through `corpus/index.csv` (`ok`, `ok-wayback` count as acquired); browser-needed.csv lists every cited URL that is `needs-browser`, `failed` or absent from the index.
