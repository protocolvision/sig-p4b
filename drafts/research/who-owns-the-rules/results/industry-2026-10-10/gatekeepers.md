## Verdict

An exchange-like chokepoint exists so far only in card payments: Visa's April 2026 rules (reported; rule text not seen) bind "Agentic Payment Providers" to consent, identity checks, scoped expiring instructions and records, Mastercard admits only registered agents with consent-bound tokens to Agent Pay, and ACP tokens carry caps and expiries and can be revoked. Elsewhere binding rules are single-platform contracts: Amazon (agents self-identify and stop on request), Google Play (no autonomous Accessibility-API agents), OpenAI's app directory (label write actions) and Copilot Studio (every agent gets an identity). For agent actions in general, model providers and clouds only prohibit misuse and offer logging, identity and kill-switch tooling as options, and even the card rules leave dispute liability open.
