# Nuclear power: fact sheet

Scope: plant automation, procedures, corrective action. Most facts are from the United States, with one UK plant example.
Compiled 2026-10-10. Grades: P = primary source opened, R = reputable secondary opened, S = search summary only. Page fetching was unavailable in this session (DNS failure on every fetch), so every fact below is graded S and should be checked against the cited page before reuse.

## D1 Autonomy and speed

The protection system covers all devices and circuitry "involved in generating those signals" that actuate reactor trip and, in a serious accident, engineered safety features (IEEE 279 scope, as cited by NRC) [1; S]. One plant's technical specifications (Duane Arnold) define reactor trip system response time as running from the sensor exceeding its trip setpoint to de-energisation of the scram pilot valve solenoids, and cap designed response time at 50 milliseconds [2; S]. Another plant's specification requires the turbine control valve fast-closure scram to initiate within 30 milliseconds of the start of valve closure [2; S]. At Three Mile Island in 1979 the reactor automatically shut down after the pressuriser relief valve opened [3; S].

## D2 Consequence of a single automated action

At Three Mile Island in 1979, the relief valve stuck open, operators reduced high-pressure injection because pressuriser level was rising, and no instrument showed the valve's actual position, only that a "close" signal had been sent [3; S]. Fuel rods were damaged and released radioactive material into the cooling water; an NRC bulletin of 5 April 1979 identified six potential human, design and mechanical failures that resulted in the core damage and radiation releases [3; S]. No example was found of a single automated plant action moving money or binding a contract (not found).

## D3 How rules are encoded

Under 10 CFR 50.55a(h), plants with construction permits issued between 1 January 1971 and 13 May 1999 must meet IEEE Std 279-1971 (or IEEE 603-1991), and applications filed on or after 13 May 1999 must meet IEEE Std 603-1991 with its 30 January 1995 correction sheet [1; S]. Plant technical specifications fix trip setpoints and the minimum number of operable trip systems and instrument channels for each reactor mode switch position, in a table (Table 3.1-1 in one plant), and require response times to be demonstrated at least once per 18 months [2; S]. At Sizewell B (UK), the digital primary protection system monitors neutron flux, nitrogen-16 power and control rod position, and a secondary protection system built from discrete analogue circuits serves as a diverse backup [4; S]. In digital modifications under 10 CFR 50.59, the contested question has been whether common-cause failure across redundant trains can be shown "sufficiently low" by qualitative assessment [5; S].

## D4 External requirements on behaviour and change

The NRC sets protection-system requirements through 10 CFR 50.55a(h) and reviews against IEEE 279 and IEEE 603 using Standard Review Plan (NUREG-0800) Appendices 7.1-B and 7.1-C [1; S]. Duke Energy's Oconee digital reactor protection and engineered safeguards system was approved by the NRC through a licence amendment on 29 January 2010 [6; S]. NEI 96-07 Appendix D, guidance for applying 10 CFR 50.59 to digital modifications, was submitted to the NRC on 30 November 2018, and draft Regulatory Guide DG-1356 (proposed Revision 2 of RG 1.187) to endorse it was issued for comment in May 2019; NRC staff had said in 2013 that existing industry guidance was inadequate [5; S]. IEC 60880:1986 covers software for computers in the safety systems of nuclear power stations, including protection systems [4; S].

## D5 Who is involved in changing a rule

Older plant technical specifications assign a Plant Operations Review Committee (PORC) to review safety-related administrative procedures and changes, and to determine whether a change involves an unreviewed safety question under 10 CFR 50.59; one specification requires PORC to meet at least once per calendar month [7; S]. Under NRC-approved Single Qualified Reviewer programmes, a qualified reviewer and a designated superintendent screen and approve procedure changes, and only changes needing a full 50.59 evaluation go to PORC [7; S]. Oconee's change to a digital protection system required a licence amendment request to the NRC [6; S].

## D6 Licensed profession

10 CFR Part 55 applies to any individual who manipulates the controls of a licensed utilisation facility [8; S]. The NRC issues two licences, reactor operator and senior reactor operator, the senior operator supervising reactor operators, after a written examination and an operating test governed by NUREG-1021 [8; S]. A national count of licensed operators was not found.

## D7 Overrides, exceptions and near misses as data

10 CFR 50 Appendix B Criterion XVI requires conditions adverse to quality to be promptly identified and corrected and, for significant conditions, the cause determined and corrective action taken to preclude repetition [9; S]. 10 CFR 50.73 requires a Licensee Event Report within 60 days of discovering a reportable event, and NRC enforcement documents cite licensees for repeat late reports as a failure of corrective action [9; S]. INPO was established in December 1979 after Three Mile Island with a remit including systematic gathering, review and analysis of operating experience at all plants; WANO was established in Moscow on 15 May 1989 after Chernobyl, and its members share operational experience through an online database under a code of confidentiality [10; S]. A typical annual count of condition reports per plant was not found.

## D8 How long in widespread use

Oconee's previous protection system was 1970s-era analogue, solid-state equipment; the digital replacement (Areva Teleperm XS, about $250 million) was installed in the three units during outages from 2011 to 2013 [6; S]. Before Oconee, individual digital safety functions existed at other US plants, including core protection calculators at Palo Verde and Waterford, load sequencers at Turkey Point and Eagle 21 systems at some Westinghouse plants, and the NRC noted that many safety-related I&C systems at licensed plants remained analogue [6; S]. Sizewell B, with a digital primary protection system on Westinghouse Eagle Series 2 equipment based on 1980s technology, synchronised to the grid on 14 February 1995 and entered commercial operation on 22 September 1995 [4; S].
