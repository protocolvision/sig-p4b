## PV: noticing deviations

- **Main structure today:** reliability coordinators staffed by NERC-certified operators.
  - Created as the "security coordinator" function in 1996 [1996 security coordinator].
  - Certification from 1998 [1998 certification], mandatory from 2007 [2007 Order 693].
- **Monitoring behind it:** compliance monitoring by Regional Entities [2007 CMEP], event analysis [2010], MIDAS [2016] and NERC alerts [2026 Level 3].
- **Seniority:** entry.
- **Lever:** an industry body, then law from 2007. Incidents were the trigger [1999 WSCC RMS; 2003 blackout].
- **Markets:** monitors arrived with the markets [1998 CAISO; 2005 ERCOT; 2008 Order 719].
- **Lags:**
  - AGC (early 1950s) to reliability coordinator: about 46 years; to mandatory monitoring: about 57.
  - First vendor EMS (1978) to EMS-outage reporting (2010): 32 years.
  - Market software to market monitors: 0 years.

## PE: designing and changing limits and settings

- **Main structure today:** mandatory NERC standards set how each entity sets its limits and settings:
  - relay loadability [2004 SPCTF]
  - model and settings verification [2014 Order 796]
  - review of remedial action schemes [2015 PRC-012-2]
  - the reliability coordinator's limit method [2022 SOL methodology]
  - line ratings [2021 Order 881]
  - inverter ride-through [2025 Order 909]
  - Committee line: NAPSIC [1963] → Operating Committee [1980] → RSTC [2019].
- **Seniority:** practitioner engineers. There is no named owner and no certification, and peer review is voluntary [2013 PSMTF].
- **Lever:** law since 2007, usually after an incident [2023 EOP-012 after Uri; 2017 IBR task force after Blue Cut; 2024 Large Loads Task Force; 2026 Iberian report].
- **Lags:**
  - AGC to voluntary guides (1963): about 11 years; to enforceable standards: about 55.
  - Inverters: first disturbance (2016) to task force: 1 year; to a standard: 9.
  - Large loads: trips to task force: 0 years.
  - Relay and remedial-action-scheme spread was not dated, so those lags are unknown.

## RD: deciding rule changes across functions

- **Main structure today:** NERC as the Electric Reliability Organization [2006], with:
  - an independent board [2001]
  - an ANSI-accredited Standards Committee [2002]
  - the RSTC [2019]
  - approval by FERC's Office of Electric Reliability, whose head is at executive level [2007 OER]
  - Europe has the equivalent in ENTSO-E and the network codes [2009; 2017].
- **Seniority:** board level [2001 NERC board; 2018 ISO-NE; 2021 ERCOT SB 2].
- **Inside utilities:**
  - The only required named person is the CIP Senior Manager (senior, required by law), and it covers cyber only [2008].
  - Executive compliance officers came from regulator guidance [2008 Policy Statement].
- **Lever:** an industry body from 1968, law from 2005. Incidents in 1965, 1996 and 2003 were the triggers.
- **Lags:**
  - AGC to NERC (1968): about 16 years; to an enforceable ERO: about 55.
  - Market software to ERCOT's legislated board: 23 years.
