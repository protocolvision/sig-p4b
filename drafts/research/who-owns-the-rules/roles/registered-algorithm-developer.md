# Registered Algorithm Developer

Covers: people registered with FINRA as Securities Traders (Series 57) because they design, develop or significantly modify algorithmic trading strategies, or supervise that work day to day. Also covers the UK "algorithmic trading" certification function under the FCA's Senior Managers and Certification Regime.

Collection method: web search results only (no pages opened). Every item is graded **S** (search summary only). Quotation marks mark words that appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

In this evidence, the US registration category first appears in 2015. FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-06 proposed it, arguing that algorithms which generate orders do work similar to individual traders, so their developers should meet the same knowledge standard. The SEC approved the change on 7 April 2016. Regulatory Notice 16-21 (June 2016) made it effective on 30 January 2017. From then, an associated person primarily responsible for designing, developing or significantly modifying an algorithmic trading strategy in equity, preferred or convertible debt securities, or who supervises or directs that work day to day, must pass the Series 57 and register as a Securities Trader (paraphrase). eFinancialCareers reported it in July 2016 as algo developers now having to register and pass an exam. In the UK, the FCA's 2016 final rules extended the Certification Regime to an "algorithmic trading" function (now SYSC 27.8.23R, item 8 in the SYSC 27.7.3R table). A law-firm summary says this captures people responsible for approving changes to an algorithm, with the "material" threshold dropped. People who make the changes are not certified but are subject to the Conduct Rules. In 2023, FIA EPTA asked HM Treasury to consider removing the algorithmic trading function. This is a registration status, not a job title. In postings it appears as a licence line ("Series 57 (Algo Trader)", "Series 57 license preferred") on senior engineering and quant roles at banks, asset managers and trading firms. Established as a regulatory category since 2017; thinly visible in job ads. Sources: FINRA RN 16-21; Norton Rose Fulbright; eFinancialCareers; FCA Handbook SYSC 27; Pinsent Masons; FIA EPTA 2023 letter (all S).

## Capability models

### FINRA Series 57: Securities Trader Representative Exam content outline
- Owner: FINRA
- Year: current outline (two-function format, in use in 2026). The earlier 2016 format came from SEC filing 34-76188 and was revised later (SEC 34-82772).
- URL: https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/qualification-exams/series57 ; outline PDF https://WWW.FINRA.ORG/sites/default/files/Series_57_Outline.pdf
- Grade: S
- Functions (current):
  - **Function 1, Trading Activities**: 41 of 50 items. Starts with 1.1 Understanding General Trading Practices. Its first task, achieving market making status, covers the floor broker's role, MPIDs, qualified block positioners, market maker registration and net capital requirements, and FINRA Rules 6220 and 6230. Later tasks were not captured.
  - **Function 2, Maintaining books and records, trade reporting, and clearance and settlement**: 9 items.
- Exam facts:
  - The SIE is a corequisite.
  - 105 minutes and a 70% pass mark (third-party figures, not confirmed on FINRA's page).
  - FINRA describes the exam as measuring the knowledge needed for "the critical functions of a securities trader".
- Superseded 2016 format (125 questions): Market Overview and Products (22); Professional Conduct and Regulatory Requirements (12); Trading Activities (79); Books and Records and Trade Reporting (12).

### FINRA Regulatory Notice 16-21: registration of algorithmic trading developers
- Owner: FINRA (rule approved by the SEC)
- Year: June 2016; effective 30 January 2017
- URL: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/16-21
- Grade: S
- Scope as listed:
  - Persons primarily responsible for the design, development or significant modification of an algorithmic trading strategy (equity, preferred, convertible debt), plus those who supervise or direct that work day to day.
  - They must pass the Series 57 and register as Securities Traders.
  - Registrants also face continuing education (Norton Rose Fulbright).
  - Rationale (RN 15-06): algorithms that generate orders do work similar to traders.

### FCA certification function: "Algorithmic trading" (SYSC 27.8.23R; table in SYSC 27.7.3R)
- Owner: Financial Conduct Authority
- Year: final rules confirmed 2016; handbook chapter updated 10/07/2026
- URL: https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/SYSC/27/7.html ; press release https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-confirms-accountability-rules-wholesale-traders-and-interim-rules-regulatory
- Grade: S
- Scope as listed:
  - An FCA certification function under s.63E FSMA. The firm must hold a certificate it has issued to each person performing it.
  - Captures people with responsibilities for algorithmic or high-frequency trading, including approving changes to an algorithm. The materiality threshold was dropped (Pinsent Masons).
  - People making changes are not captured but are subject to the Conduct Rules.
  - Applies to banks, building societies and PRA-designated investment firms (FCA 2016).
- The exact rule text of SYSC 27.8.23R was not retrieved.

### FIX Trading Community: Recommended Practices for Algorithm Testing and Certification (and extension EP292)
- Owner: FIX Trading Community (Algorithmic Trading Working Group)
- Year: not captured (Mondo Visione URL suggests 2026)
- URL: https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/fix-releases-new-guidelines-on-algo-testing-and-certification-202663/ ; EP292 https://fixtrading.org/packages/ep292
- Grade: S
- Content as listed:
  - Setting system configurations.
  - Executing and orchestrating test runs across platforms.
  - "formalising production readiness through standardised certificates".
  - EP292 supports EU algo-certification requirements, under which firms must certify their algos to venues and provide test results.
- Note: this certifies algorithms, not people.

Cross-references: RTS 6 Art. 3 staff knowledge and PRA SS5/18 algorithm approval sign-offs are recorded in `etrading-risk-and-control.md` and also apply to developers.
