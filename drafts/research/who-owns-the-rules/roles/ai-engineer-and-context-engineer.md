# AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Context Engineer (successors of Prompt Engineer)

Collected October 2026 from web search results only (page fetching was blocked). Grade **S** = search-result summary only; no page was opened. Quotation marks are used only where the words appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

"Prompt engineer" was the 2023 title: Fortune (9 March 2023) reported "over $300,000 for a new kind of job", Anthropic posted "Prompt Engineer & Librarian" on Lever, and Boston Children's Hospital hired its first AI prompt engineer in 2023. "AI Engineer" as a distinct discipline was popularized by Shawn "swyx" Wang's essay "The Rise of the AI Engineer" (Latent Space, June 2023) and the AI Engineer Summit (2023), framing a role at the intersection of software engineering and AI built on model APIs rather than research teams. By 2026 "AI Engineer" and "Applied AI Engineer" are common titles (Anthropic, startups on Greenhouse and VC boards), with evals, agents and production ownership at the core. "Context engineering" appears in June 2025 (Walden Yan of Cognition, 12 June; Tobi Lütke, 18 or 19 June; Andrej Karpathy, about 25 June), was named a Gartner data and analytics trend in July 2025, and is said to have been formalized in an Anthropic post "Effective context engineering for AI agents" in September 2025 (unverified in results). "Context Engineer" postings are still sparse but real in 2026 (BT Group, Oxford University Press, Hex). Boston Children's own prompt engineer said by 2024 the work had moved from detailed prompts to building pipelines that connect LLMs into products.

### 1. Anthropic, "Applied AI Engineer" (variants: Digital Natives Business, Beneficial Deployments, Tokyo)
- Date: mid-2026 (some copies expired). Seniority: 4+ years in technical roles. Location: San Francisco, New York, Tokyo and others; occasional travel to customer sites. Pay (Digital Natives variant): $200K–$320K.
- URL: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5055488008 (Greenhouse, employer board); https://jobs.accel.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/81748657-applied-ai-engineer (aggregator). Grade: S.
- Responsibilities:
  - Act as a technical Product Engineer and trusted technical advisor to customers adopting Claude (paraphrase).
  - Design custom pilots, prototypes and evaluation frameworks for customer deployments (paraphrase).
  - Guide customers from technical discovery to successful deployment; work with account executives on product requirements (paraphrase).
  - Lead hands-on technical workshops and code reviews with customer engineering teams (paraphrase).
  - Write documentation on prompting, evaluation, agentic and architecture techniques (paraphrase).
  - Stay current on prompt engineering, agent frameworks and retrieval systems (paraphrase).
  - Identify common design patterns and "contribute insights back to our Product and Engineering teams."
- Requirements:
  - 4+ years in technical roles such as Customer Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Software Engineer or Technical Product Manager (paraphrase).
  - Strong Python/TypeScript (paraphrase).

### Historical comparison H2. Boston Children's Hospital, "AI Prompt Engineer"
- 2023. The original posting was not found; the role is known through reporting on the hire (Dinesh Rai, MD). Seniority: clinician with LLM/NLP coding experience. Location: Boston.
- URL: https://childrenshospitals.org/news/childrens-hospitals-today/2024/08/the-newest-role-in-childrens-hospitals (trade association article, August 2024); https://www.darkdaily.com/tag/llm/ (relaying MedPage Today). Grade: S.
- Responsibilities (as reported):
  - Design inputs (prompts) for generative AI tools to produce specific, high-quality outputs (paraphrase).
  - By 2024: build pipelines connecting LLMs and assemble them into products; AI chat for medical education; background tasks analyzing medical notes (paraphrase).
  - Rai: "As these models get better, it's less about actual prompt engineering... The need for very detailed specific prompts has lessened over time."
- Requirements (as reported):
  - Clinical background plus experience coding for LLMs and NLP; described as "a bit of a unicorn-type candidate."

## Capability models

No published "context engineer" framework was found. The available models are vendor certification exam guides for AI application developers.

1. **AWS Certified Generative AI Developer – Professional (AIP-C01)**. Owner: Amazon Web Services. Year: 2025–2026. URL: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-professional-01/ai-professional-01.md (official guide; weights from third parties: Tutorials Dojo, KodeKloud, Educative). Grade: S.
   - Domain 1: Foundation Model Integration, Data Management, and Compliance (31%).
   - Domain 2: Implementation and Integration (26%).
   - Domain 3: AI Safety, Security, and Governance (20%).
   - Domain 4: Operational Efficiency and Optimization for GenAI Applications (12%).
   - Domain 5: Testing, Validation, and Troubleshooting (11%).
2. **Microsoft Exam AI-103, Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate** (successor to AI-102). Owner: Microsoft. Year: skills outline 16 April 2026. URL: https://tutorialsdojo.com/ai-103-azure-ai-app-and-agent-developer-associate-study-guide/ (third-party; official outline not retrieved). Grade: S.
   - Plan and manage an Azure AI solution (25–30%): model choice, Foundry architecture, scaling, cost, monitoring.
   - Implement generative AI and agentic solutions (30–35%): RAG pipelines, agents with memory and tools, multi-agent workflows, tool orchestration with safeguards.
   - Implement computer vision solutions (10–15%).
   - Implement text analysis solutions (weight not shown).
   - Implement information extraction solutions (weight not shown).
3. **Microsoft Exam AI-102, Azure AI Engineer Associate** (retired 30 June 2026 per a third-party guide). Owner: Microsoft. Year: skills measured as of 23 December 2025. URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-102. Grade: S. Third-party weights conflict; one lists Plan and manage an Azure AI solution (20–25%), Implement generative AI solutions (15–20%), Implement an agentic solution (5–10%); remaining domains (vision, NLP, information extraction) not shown consistently.
4. **Definition sources for context engineering** (not competency models): Lütke's "the art of providing all the context for the task to be plausibly solvable by the LLM"; Karpathy's "the delicate art and science of filling the context window with just the right information for the next step" (both as quoted in towardsai.net / jiangren.com.au results); Anthropic, "Effective context engineering for AI agents" (Sept 2025, not retrieved).
