# AI Governance Lead

Covers: Head of AI Governance, AI Governance Manager/Lead, AI Risk Officer, Responsible AI Lead at companies that use AI but do not build frontier models.

Collection method: web search results only (no pages opened). Every item is graded **S** (search summary only). Quotation marks mark words that appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

In this evidence, the role first appears as a defined profession in 2023. IAPP released the AI Governance Professional (AIGP) body of knowledge in June 2023 (eDiscovery Today, 23 June 2023). IAPP announced the AIGP exam on 5 March 2024, held the first sitting in April 2024, and described the credential as built "for the emerging AI governance profession". By 2025 the function was widespread: IAPP's AI Governance Profession Report (16 April 2025, with Credo AI, based on more than 670 respondents) found 77% of surveyed organisations working on AI governance, rising to near 90% of those already using AI. The same report notes a shortage of qualified AI governance talent. Over 2025–2026 the role was formalised further. The AIGP body of knowledge was revised twice (v2.0.1 effective 3 February 2025, v2.1 effective 2 February 2026). ISACA added two AI credentials, AAIA (audit) and AAISM (security management). IAPP published an AI Governance Skills Framework that profiles roles, and for its professional profiles IAPP collected public job profiles that explicitly called for AI governance work "throughout 2026". Titles are not standardised, and seniority runs from analyst to Managing Director. Postings in the evidence date from 2025–2026. The role is emergent but consolidating fast, especially in banking, insurance, healthcare, defence and government. Sources: IAPP AIGP launch release; IAPP AI Governance Profession Report 2025; IAPP AIGSF pages; ISACA AAIA/AAISM outline pages (all S).

## Capability models

### IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP) Body of Knowledge
- Owner: IAPP
- Year: v1 released June 2023; v2.0.1 effective 3 February 2025; v2.1 effective 2 February 2026
- URL: https://iapp.org/certify/body-of-knowledge-aigp
- Grade: S
- Domains:
  - v2.0.1 condensed the domains "from seven to four" (IAPP Q1 2025 update).
  - Domain titles were not confirmed from IAPP. A third party (VerifyWise) lists: AI Governance Foundations; Risk Management and Compliance; Technical and Operational Controls; Organizational Integration.
  - Secondary sources say v2.1 adds agentic AI, third-party risk assessments, ISO 42005 and automated decision-making rules.
- Exam: 100 questions, 3 hours.

### ISACA Advanced in AI Audit (AAIA) exam content outline
- Owner: ISACA
- Year: not captured (current in 2026)
- URL: https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aaia/aaia-exam-content-outline
- Grade: S
- Domains:
  - **Domain 1, AI Governance and Risk (33%)**, with subdomains A AI Models, Considerations, and Requirements; B AI Governance and Program Management; C AI Risk Management; D Privacy and Data Governance Programs; E Leading Practices, Ethics, Regulations, and Standards for AI.
  - **Domain 2, AI Operations (46%)**. Per a third party, it covers data management, the AI development lifecycle, change management, supervision, AI-specific testing, threats and vulnerabilities, and incident response.
  - **Domain 3, AI Auditing Tools and Techniques (21%)**. The weight comes from a third party only.
- Exam: 90 questions. A third party gives the prerequisite as an active CISA or equivalent.

### ISACA Advanced in AI Security Management (AAISM) exam content outline
- Owner: ISACA
- Year: not captured
- URL: https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aaism/aaism-exam-content-outline
- Grade: S
- Domains:
  - **Domain 1, AI Governance and Program Management (31%)**: stakeholder considerations and regulatory requirements; AI-related strategies and policies; AI asset and data life cycle management; security program development; business continuity; incident response.
  - **Domain 2, AI Risk Management (31%)**: risk assessments, risk thresholds, threat management, vulnerability management, vendors and supply chains (detail from third parties).
  - **Domain 3, AI Technologies and Controls (38%)**: AI security architecture, model lifecycle, tailored controls. Title and weight are from third parties; one third party gives 31/34/35 instead.
- Exam: 90 questions.

### IAPP AI Governance Skills Framework (AIGSF)
- Owner: IAPP
- Year: 2026 (Part II built from job profiles collected "throughout 2026"; Part I drew on IAPP surveys 2023–2026)
- URL: https://iapp.org/resources/article/ai-governance-skills-framework ; profiles: https://iapp.org/resources/article/ai-governance-skills-framework-professional-profiles ; assurance: https://iapp.org/resources/article/ai-governance-skills-framework-profiles-assurance
- Grade: S
- Structure:
  - Part I, Executive Functions: the leaders accountable for AI governance oversight.
  - Part II, Professional Profiles, in eight types: policy, governance, technical, product, ethics, risk, legal, assurance.
- Profile descriptions in the results:
  - Governance profiles build "the processes, committees, inventories, risk registers, and accountability structures".
  - Technical profiles develop controls, assessments, monitoring and compliance capabilities.
  - Product profiles guide AI risk and lifecycle management through roadmaps.
  - Legal profiles cover policy, regulatory compliance and risk assessment.
  - IAPP expects the assurance category to mature most in the next version.

### SFIA: AI governance and assurance mapping
- Owner: SFIA Foundation
- Year: not captured
- URL: https://sfia-online.org/en/tools-and-resources/ai-skills-framework/ai-assurance-and-governance/sfia-skills-ai-governance-and-assurance
- Grade: S
- Content: applies existing SFIA skills to the AI domain rather than inventing new skills. It sets out four role archetypes, including an AI governance and assurance role in the second line that defines control objectives, policy requirements and risk frameworks.

### BCS AI assurance professional skills framework (in development)
- Owner: BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT (UK)
- Year: in progress (first-year deliverable)
- URL: https://www.bcs.org/the-home-of-the-ai-professional/ai-assurance-leading-a-new-profession-responsibly/
- Grade: S
- Content: a planned skills and competencies framework for AI assurance professionals, alongside a voluntary, government-backed code of ethics and conduct. Domains are not yet published in the results.
