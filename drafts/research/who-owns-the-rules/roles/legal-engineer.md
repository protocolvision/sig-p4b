# Legal Engineer (Legal Engineer, Senior / Principal Legal Engineer, Legal Knowledge Engineer, Legal Workflow Engineer)

Collected October 2026 from web search results only (page fetching was blocked). Grade **S** = search-result summary only; no page was opened. Quotation marks are used only where the words appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

The older form is "legal knowledge engineer": Artificial Lawyer (December 2017) reports that Pinsent Masons used it "at least as early as 2014", adopting a term it associates with Richard Susskind (no primary Susskind source surfaced). InfoLaw (December 2017) finds the earliest apt use of "legal engineer" in an October 2012 comment on Stephen Mayson's blog, and notes a Legal Process Engineer at Inksters Solicitors from 2014 who called herself "Legal Engineer". Stuart Barr (HighQ) wrote "The Rise of the Legal Engineer" in 2016, describing an interface between legal experts and technology experts; a lawyer tried to trademark the term in 2017. Law360 Pulse (via MLex, 2025–2026) reports the title is most common at legal-technology vendors and some law firms and is entering corporate legal teams, usually meaning process and workflow building with legal tech rather than writing code. The generative-AI wave has made it a common, well-paid title (Harvey's Legal Engineering organization across AMER, EMEA and APAC; Latham & Watkins' AI & Legal Innovation team with Legal Engineering Attorneys, Legal Knowledge Engineers and Legal Workflow Engineers; Everlaw, Consilio, Husch Blackwell). No posting titled "Principal Legal Engineer" surfaced.

## Capability models

No framework, exam or ladder written for the legal engineer itself was found. The closest are a general legal competency model that includes technology and process, and university units on legal automation and legal knowledge engineering.

1. **The Delta Model (legal professional competencies)**. Owner: Thomson Reuters Legal Executive Institute (white paper by Runyon and Carrel, 2019); also published in MIT Computational Law Report. Year: 2019. URL: https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/insights/white-papers/delta-model; https://law.mit.edu/pub/deltamodellawyer. Grade: S.
   - The Law (legal knowledge and skills).
   - Business & Operations: tech savvy, data analytics, project management; top competencies Business Fundamentals and Project Management & Workflow.
   - Personal Effectiveness: relationship management, entrepreneurial mindset, emotional intelligence, communication, character; top competencies Entrepreneurial Mindset and Adaptability.
   - The mix shifts with the role; tested with 45 professionals, mostly hiring managers.
2. **LAWS13019 Legal Automation (unit profile)**. Owner: CQUniversity (Australia). Year: 2022–2027 profiles. URL: https://handbook.cqu.edu.au/facet/unit-profiles/profile/LAWS13019/2027/HT1. Grade: S. Topics: legal project management; expert systems; document and process automation; data analytics; machine learning; blockchain; students build a legal app through legal knowledge engineering; no programming required.
3. **LAWS3196 / JURD7596 Designing Technology Solutions for Access to Justice** (successor of JURD7332 Computer Applications to Law). Owner: UNSW Sydney. Year: 2010–2025 handbooks. URL: https://handbook.unsw.edu.au/postgraduate/courses/2025/jurd7596. Grade: S. Topics: legal information systems combining expert systems, hypertext and text retrieval; document generation from precedents; group build for a not-for-profit partner.
4. **LIFTED (Legal Industry Framework for Training and Education)**. Owner: Singapore Academy of Law. Year unknown. URL: https://sal.org.sg/learn/lifted/lifted-components/. Grade: S. Components: career maps, skills maps, competency statements at basic, intermediate and advanced levels; aimed at disputes and corporate lawyers and in-house counsel, not technologists.
