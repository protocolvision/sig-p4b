# GRC Engineer (Governance, Risk and Compliance Engineer; Compliance Automation Engineer)

Collected October 2026 from web search results only (page fetching was blocked). Grade **S** = search-result summary only; no page was opened. Quotation marks are used only where the words appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

No dated first use was found. A Sprinto newsletter piece (2025–2026) says that "five years ago" GRC engineer was not a title most compliance leaders would have recognized, that it is now common on job boards, conference tracks and org charts, and that the discipline grew from late-2010s pressures by applying DevOps practice to GRC (policies as code, evidence generated during operations, controls built into deployment). A GRC Engineer podcast/Substack launched in October 2024 "championing the engineering movement". The "GRC Engineering Manifesto" was written by leaders at Vanta and Lovable (Vanta webinar page, July 2026); its text and date did not surface in search (the grc.engineering site was not indexed), so its values are recorded here only through secondhand framing. University events (UMGC, "Rise of GRC Engineering", November 2025) and vendor content (Anecdotes, Vanta, Sprinto) show the term is established. Postings in 2025–2026 come from security-heavy software firms and federal-market sellers (ID.me, Cursor, Smartsheet, Vanta, WorkOS, Alteryx, Nintendo of America, Charlie Health, Lantern), often with variants "GRC Security Engineer", "Compliance Automation Engineer", "AI GRC Engineer", "GRC Systems Engineer".

## Capability models

1. **GRC Engineering Manifesto**. Owner: leaders at Vanta and Lovable (individuals not named in results). Year: not found (cited by July 2026). URL: https://grc.engineering (not indexed; not retrieved); referenced at https://www.vanta.com/webinars/what-is-grc-engineering-a-fresh-take-on-an-old-space-apj. Grade: S. Stated values not retrieved. Secondhand framing only: GRC engineering replaces checkbox compliance and manual toil with automation, systems thinking and "outcomes that actually matter" (Vanta page). Related practitioner framing (LinkedIn, GRC Engineering Club member): ask whether a control can run automatically, prevent rather than detect, and generate evidence as work happens. **Gap: manifesto text not obtained.**
2. **ISC2 Certified in Governance, Risk and Compliance (CGRC) exam outline**. Owner: ISC2. Year: effective 15 June 2024. URL: https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cgrc/cgrc-certification-exam-outline. Grade: S.
   - Domain 1: Security and Privacy Governance, Risk Management, and Compliance Program (16%).
   - Domain 2: Scope of the System (10%).
   - Domain 3: Selection and Approval of Framework, Security, and Privacy Controls (14%).
   - Domain 4: Implementation of Security and Privacy Controls (17%).
   - Domain 5: Assessment/Audit of Security and Privacy Controls (16%).
   - Domain 6: System Compliance (14%) (the authorization decision).
   - Domain 7: Compliance Maintenance (13%) (formerly Continuous Monitoring).
3. **ISACA Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC) exam content outline**. Owner: ISACA. Year: effective 3 November 2025. URL: https://www.isaca.org/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2025/isaca-updates-cdpse-and-crisc-exams-to-reflect-latest-risk-and-privacy-priorities (weights via InfoSecTrain and CertCrush). Grade: S.
   - Governance (26%): organizational and risk governance, business context, risk appetite and culture.
   - IT Risk Assessment (22%): identify, analyze and evaluate IT risk scenarios; new subsections on LLM and AI vulnerabilities.
   - Risk Response and Reporting (32%).
   - Information Technology and Security (20%).
4. **GRC Engineering Masterclass**. Owner: "Cloud Security Guy" (Substack author). Year unknown (2025–2026). URL: https://cloudsecurityguy.substack.com/p/why-i-created-the-grc-engineering. Grade: S. Topics: turning ISO controls into AWS Config rules; automating evidence collection; Python, Terraform and policy-as-code for continuous compliance; project-based modules (e.g. finding users without MFA).
5. **Implementation-Grade GRC Engineering** (commercial course). Owner: The Art of Service. Year unknown. URL: https://store.theartofservice.com/grc-engineering-impl-9f3k2-a96e8/. Grade: S. 12 modules; the compliance-as-code module covers defining controls in configuration files, linting infrastructure policies and integrating with pull-request workflows. Templated catalogue; low confidence.
