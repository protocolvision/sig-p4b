# AI agent operations specialist

People who run live AI agents in customer service, IT or sales operations. Titles seen: AI Agent(s) Operations Specialist, AI Agent Ops Specialist, AI Operations Specialist, AI Ops Specialist, Sr. AI Operations Specialist (Sales Operations), AI Conversation Designer, ServiceNow Now Assist consultant/administrator.

Collected 10 October 2026 from web search result summaries only (no page was opened). Every item is graded **S** (search summary only). Quotation marks mark wording that appeared in a search result; the search tool summarises pages, so even quoted words may be the summariser's rather than the employer's and should be checked against the posting before citing.

## Status

The evidence is recent and thin on dates. The oldest dated signal for the capability model is Salesforce's 2024 renaming of its AI Specialist exam to Agentforce Specialist (Salesforce Admins blog, 2024); the dated postings all fall in 2026: Notion's AI Conversation Designer, Customer Support (about June 2026), the Databricks family of "AI Operations Specialist" postings (the senior Sales Operations variant is dated 2 October 2026 on an aggregator), and undated but current listings at Mobisystems, EarnIn and a staffing-agency contract ("AI Agent Ops Specialist", Lehi, UT). The title is not settled: one search summary found "no single standard definition for the title", and trend commentary lists a loose set of neighbouring names (Agent Supervisor, AI Ops Manager, Agent Operator) with salary bands of $95K–$200K+ from blog sources (aicodex.to, 2026). "Conversation designer" appears in lists of emerging UX roles and is described as covering the instruction layer for agents (prompts, system prompts, guardrails). No primary labour-market dataset (LinkedIn Economic Graph, Indeed Hiring Lab) for these exact titles was found. Commonness: a handful of employers per title in the results; looks uncommon but recurring, with Databricks alone running at least four regional and seniority variants.

## Capability models

### A. Salesforce Certified Agentforce Specialist — exam guide
- Owner: Salesforce (Trailhead). Year: 2024 (renamed from AI Specialist), revised 2025 and for Spring '26. Official URL not surfaced; secondary: https://www.certcrush.app/blog/agentforce-specialist-certification-2026-cost-pass-mark ; https://k2university.com/news/the-salesforce-agentforce-specialist-exam-has-evolved-whats-new-and-different/ ; https://admin.salesforce.com/blog/2024/prepare-for-the-salesforce-ai-specialist-exam-and-agentforce . Grade S.
- Domains (Spring '26 version per a 2026 secondary source): AI Agents 35%; Prompt Engineering 20%; Data 360 Fundamentals 20%; Testing, Deployment and Maintenance 10%; Governance and Observability 10%; Multi-Agent Orchestration 5%.
- Earlier (October 2025) version: AI Agents 35%; Prompt Engineering 20%; Data Cloud for Agentforce 20%; Development Lifecycle 20%; Multi-Agent Interoperability 5% (MCP and agent-to-agent protocol). Data Cloud objectives include retrievers (individual and ensemble) and keyword/vector/hybrid search.
- Sources disagree on weights, pass mark (72% or 73%) and fee; the official Trailhead guide was not found.

### B. Microsoft Exam AB-620: Designing and Building Integrated AI Agent Solutions in Copilot Studio (AI Agent Builder Associate)
- Owner: Microsoft. Year: 2026 (beta; GA planned June 2026, not confirmed). URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-620 . Grade S.
- Domains (weights from third-party guides): Plan and configure agent solutions 30–35% (enterprise system integrations, identity strategy, channels and deployment, environment variables); Integrate and extend agents in Copilot Studio 40–45%; Test and manage agents 20–25%.
