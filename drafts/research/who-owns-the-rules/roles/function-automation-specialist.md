# Function automation specialist

People embedded in one business function (People/HR, Finance & Accounting, Revenue Operations) who build and run agents and automations for that function. Titles seen: People Operations & Automation Specialist, People Operations and AI Partner, AI & Automation Specialist - People Operations, People Operations AI & Automation Manager, HRIS Automation Specialist, AI Automation Engineer (Finance & Accounting), GTM AI Agent Builder, GTM Engineering and Systems Manager, Admin & AI Architect (RevOps), RevOps Specialist - AI Performance Enhancement, AI Business Automation Engineer.

Collected 10 October 2026 from web search result summaries only (no page was opened). Every item is graded **S** (search summary only). Quotation marks mark wording that appeared in a search result; the search tool summarises pages, so quoted words may be the summariser's and should be checked against the posting.

## Status

All dated postings in the evidence are from 2026: Yahoo's AI & Automation Specialist - People Operations (13 January 2026), Talkdesk's People Operations AI & Automation Manager (11 May 2026) and Pencil's People Operations and AI Partner (late August 2026). The Finance (Cengage) and RevOps (Gusto, Ashby, Lotlinx, Veesion, dbt Labs) postings are undated in the results but current. Box's CEO is reported describing "internal automation engineers who rewire how the company works", and Box reportedly posted an AI Business Automation Engineer role at USD 146,500–183,000 (WebProNews; Yahoo Finance/Forbes roundup, 2026). Commonness: the role is visible in many employers under many titles, but no title is settled; the exact "People Operations & Automation Specialist" title surfaced once (Bloomerang), and a trend search found no source using the exact title "AI automation specialist". Function-specific suffixes ("- People Operations", "Finance & Accounting", "GTM") are the common pattern. No primary labour-market count was found.

No function-specific competency framework for "people who build agents inside a function" was found. The models below cover the agent/automation half (vendor certifications) or the function half (professional bodies).

### A. UiPath Agentic Automation Associate and Agentic Automation Professional
- Owner: UiPath. Year: 2025–2026 (exact date not in results). URL: https://academy.uipath.com/certifications . Grade S.
- Associate: "foundational skills for collaborating with AI agents in process automation"; suits technical and non-technical roles.
- Professional: design, build and govern automation solutions "that orchestrate autonomous agents, automation routines, enterprise systems, and human decision-making"; apply governance practices for scalability, observability and responsible AI use.
- Domain list and weights: in the Exam Description Document, not captured.

### B. Workato Academy: Introduction to Agent Studio; Automation Pro I and II
- Owner: Workato. Year unknown. URLs: https://systematic.workato.com/t5/highlights-hub/course-of-the-month-introduction-to-agent-studio/m-p/11490 ; https://www.workato.com/demos/live-training . Grade S.
- Agent Studio course topics: Genies; job descriptions and prompts; knowledge bases; custom skills and skill recipes. Ends with a certification option (details cut off).
- Automation Pro I (beginner, first recipe) and II (intermediate): trigger conditions, data mapping, conditional actions.

### C. Microsoft Exam AB-730: AI Business Professional
- Owner: Microsoft. Year: 2026 (skills measured as of 22 July 2026; new version from 20 October 2026). Secondary URLs: https://tutorialsdojo.com/ab-730-microsoft-certified-ai-business-professional-study-guide/ ; https://vladtalkstech.com/ab-730-study-guide-ai-business-professional/ . Grade S.
- Areas (sources disagree on three vs four domains): understand generative AI fundamentals; manage prompts and conversations with AI; draft and analyse business content with AI; responsible AI and data protection; Microsoft 365 Copilot agents. Weights reported as 25–30%, 35–40%, 25–30%.
