# Clinical Informaticist

Covers: Clinical Informaticist, Nurse/Nursing Informaticist (Informatics Nurse Specialist), Chief Medical Information/Informatics Officer (CMIO), and clinical decision support (CDS) or alert-governance roles in hospitals and health systems.

Collection method: web search results only (no pages opened). Every item is graded **S** (search summary only). Quotation marks mark words that appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

The role is well established, with board certification for physicians and nurses. The AMIA Board approved the "Core Content for the Subspecialty of Clinical Informatics" in November 2008, and it was published in JAMIA in March–April 2009 (Gardner et al.). ABMS approved clinical informatics as a subspecialty open to diplomates of all member boards; the date is given as 2010 in a 2018 Yearbook article and as 21 September 2011 by AMIA. ABPM and the American Board of Pathology ran the first board exam in October 2013, and 455 physicians received the first certificates in January 2014. In July 2018, AMIA, ABPM and ABPath ran a formal practice analysis, published by Silverman et al. in 2019, which produced 5 domains, 42 tasks and 139 knowledge and skill statements and added data analytics and governance. ABPM switched to an updated content outline in 2021. On the nursing side, ANCC runs an Informatics Nursing board certification: a 2022 role delineation study (Exam 27), and a test content outline effective 3 May 2023, republished on 29 August 2025. The CMIO is described in a 2010s-era industry piece as "a fairly new addition to the healthcare industry". CDS and alert governance shows up as committee work led by informaticists, for example Cleveland Clinic's CDS Governance Workgroup led by Associate CMIOs and Vanderbilt's "BPA Clickbusters" (2022). ANCC's informatics nursing certification start date was **not** confirmed in these searches. Sources: AMIA history; Gardner 2009; Silverman 2019; ABPM; ANCC; postings below (all S).

## Capability models

### AMIA Core Content for the Subspecialty of Clinical Informatics (Gardner et al.)
- Owner: AMIA
- Year: approved November 2008; published 2009 (JAMIA 16(2):153–157)
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2649325 ; https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/LHC-publications/pubs/Corecontentforthesubspecialtyofclinicalinformatics.html
- Grade: S
- Domains:
  - Fundamentals.
  - Clinical Decision Making and Care Process Improvement.
  - Health Information Systems.
  - Leadership and Management of Change.
- Older exam weights per a 2018-era article: 10% / 30% / 40% / 20% (superseded).

### Clinical informatics subspecialty practice analysis: Domains, tasks, and knowledge (Silverman et al.)
- Owner: AMIA with ABPM and ABPath
- Year: survey July 2018; published 2019 (JAMIA, doi 10.1093/jamia/ocz051)
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7647208
- Grade: S
- Structure: 5 domains, 42 tasks, 139 knowledge and skill statements; 316 of 1,695 diplomates responded. It adds a practice domain, tasks and statements on data analytics and governance, and describes practice as tasks as well as knowledge. Exact domain titles were not captured. It feeds the ACGME Clinical Informatics Milestones (Supplemental Guide).

### ABPM Clinical Informatics Exam Content Outline
- Owner: American Board of Preventive Medicine (with ABPath)
- Year: updated outline in use from 2021; 2026 Longitudinal Assessment Program sections listed
- URL: https://www.theabpm.org/clinical-informatics-content-outline/ ; LAP: https://www.theabpm.org/continuing-certification-program-ccp/clinical-informatics-longitudinal-assessment-program-lap/
- Grade: S
- Domains:
  - I. Fundamental Knowledge and Skills (20%).
  - II. Improving Care Delivery and Outcomes (30%).
  - Domains III onward were not captured.

### ANCC Informatics Nursing Board Certification Test Content Outline
- Owner: American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC)
- Year: effective 3 May 2023 (file posted 29 August 2025); role delineation study executive summary 17 February 2022
- URL: https://prd.healthynursehealthynation.org/globalassets/informatics-tco_08292025-for-webposting.pdf ; RDS: https://anaprodsite1.nursingworld.org/globalassets/certification/certification-specialty-pages/resources/role-delineation-studies/exam-27-informatics-nursing-rds-executive-summary-final-2022-02-17.pdf
- Grade: S
- Structure:
  - 150 questions (125 scored, 25 pretest).
  - Domains and percentages were not captured. Several mirror PDFs listing domains (e.g. "Foundations of Nursing Informatics" ~20–25%) look unreliable and are not used.

### AMIA 2017 Core Competencies for Applied Health Informatics Education at the Master's Degree Level (Valenta et al.)
- Owner: AMIA (Accreditation Committee); used by CAHIIM
- Year: 2018 (JAMIA 25(12):1657–1668)
- URL: https://pure.lib.usf.edu/en/publications/amia-board-white-paper-amia-2017-core-competencies-for-applied-he-2/ ; https://cahiim.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/hi-ms-curriculum.pdf
- Grade: S
- Foundational domains:
  - F1 Health.
  - F2 Information Science and Technology.
  - F3 Social and Behavioral Science.
  - F4 Health Information Science and Technology.
  - F5 Human Factors and Socio-technical Systems.
  - F6 Social and Behavioral Aspects of Health.
  - F7 Social, Behavioral, and Information Science and Technology Applied to Health.
  - F8 Professionalism.
  - F9 Interpersonal Collaborative Practice (as given in the search result).
  - F10 Leadership.
- Johnson et al. (2023) map these domains to AMIA's delineation of practice for health informatics.
