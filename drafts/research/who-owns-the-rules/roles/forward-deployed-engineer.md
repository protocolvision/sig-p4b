# Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), Forward Deployed AI Engineer, Deployment Strategist

Collected October 2026 from web search results only (page fetching was blocked). Grade **S** = search-result summary only; no page was opened. Quotation marks are used only where the words appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

The evidence ties the title to Palantir, which describes itself as having "pioneered this unique position, embedding talented engineers directly with our customers" (Palantir posting text reproduced on an aggregator; S); a vendor guide says Palantir coined the term and built the operating model around it, pairing the Forward Deployed Software Engineer with a non-engineering twin, the Deployment Strategist (Oxagile; S). No dated first use was found in the results. The title has since spread to AI model and platform firms: OpenAI, Anthropic (inside its Applied AI team, with a Head and Managers of Forward Deployed Engineering), Scale AI (Director and Sr. Director of FDE, plus an "AI Deployment Strategist"), and Salesforce (Agentforce FDEs working alongside Deployment Strategists). It is now common and fragmenting: a vendor snapshot dated 8 September 2026 counts 77 of Palantir's 310 open roles as forward deployed, with software, infrastructure, AI, enablement, reliability, security and site-reliability variants (Valletta Software; S, unverified method). Consultancies (PwC, UK recruiters) now post "Forward Deployed AI Engineer – Palantir Foundry" roles. No industry-wide standard or published employer career ladder was found.

### 1. Palantir Technologies, "Forward Deployed Software Engineer"
- Date unknown (several live and archived copies). Seniority: entry to mid; aggregator copies state about 1–3 years depending on level. Location: many (US, UK, EU; some require clearance or local language). Travel commonly 25–50%.
- URL: https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/98b81271-9e15-40b7-8dce-0c62f24702f9 (Lever, employer board). Aggregator copies: jointaro.com. Grade: S.
- Responsibilities:
  - Mix of architecture discussions with engineers, large-scale data work, custom web application development, executive conversations and team strategy (paraphrase).
  - Work directly with customers to understand their hardest problems quickly, then design and implement data-driven solutions, often on site (paraphrase).
  - Wrangle massive-scale data and use AI to accelerate critical operations (paraphrase, aggregator).
  - Engage customer stakeholders from technical teams to executives (paraphrase, aggregator).
  - Own high-stakes projects end to end in small teams with minimal supervision, described as similar to a startup CTO (paraphrase).
- Requirements:
  - Programming in Python, Java, TypeScript or JavaScript (paraphrase, aggregator).
  - About 1–3 years of experience depending on level (paraphrase, aggregator).
  - Willingness to travel 25–50% (paraphrase).
  - For some locations: security clearance or local-language fluency (paraphrase).

## Capability models

No published employer competency framework or career ladder was found. What exists is commercial certification and training, plus a hint that employers keep internal frameworks.

1. **Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE)**. Owner: Global Skill Development Council (GSDC). Year unknown. URL: https://www.gsdcouncil.org/certification-program/what-is-forward-deployed-engineer. Grade: S. The GSDC page snippet showed only the title. A competitor blog (FDE Academy) describes a 13-module syllabus covering: production Python and API design; LLMs; RAG systems; agentic workflows; cloud deployment; client engagement. Module names not available.
2. **Certified Forward Deployed Engineer (CFDE)**. Owner: ADaSci (per Scaler). Year unknown. URL: https://www.scaler.com/topics/forward-deployed-engineer-certifications/ (secondary). Grade: S. Described only as a 30-hour, self-paced, project-driven certification aimed at existing developers; no domains listed.
3. **FDE Academy curriculum**. Owner: FDE Academy (commercial). Year 2026. URL: https://fde.academy/blog/fde-academy-method-forward-deployed-engineer-training. Grade: S. Claims to be grounded in "160+ FDE roles"; selection includes a proficiency test and technical interview. Domains not listed in the snippet; a sibling page lists FDE skills (https://fde.academy/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-skills), contents not shown.
4. **Illustrative FDE career progression**. Owner: one commentary source in the result set (not pinned to a URL; likely https://www.secondtalent.com/occupations/forward-deployed-engineer/). Grade: S. Forward Deployed Engineer → Senior FDE → Principal FDE → Technical Solutions Architect → VP of Solutions Engineering / Engineering. Not authoritative.
5. **Evidence of internal frameworks**. A Palantir "Forward Deployed Program Trainer" posting asks for someone to translate "competency frameworks and career ladder expectations into measurable learning objectives" (https://www.tealhq.com/job/forward-deployed-program-trainer_7ea1a583a23e88990e460476d16bf9b0d2746; aggregator; S). The frameworks themselves are not public.
