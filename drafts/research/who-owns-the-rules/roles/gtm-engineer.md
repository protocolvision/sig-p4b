# GTM Engineer (Go-to-Market Engineer)

Collected October 2026 from web search results only (page fetching was blocked). Grade **S** = search-result summary only; no page was opened. Quotation marks are used only where the words appeared in a search result; everything else is paraphrase.

## Status

Sources consistently credit Clay (the data-enrichment and outbound workflow vendor) with the title. One guide, written in partnership with Clay, says Clay formulated "GTM engineering" in 2023 in an internal Slack discussion, and that Clay's own "Complete Guide to GTM Engineering" popularized it (nextplayso Substack; S); other write-ups say Clay "effectively invented" the role (S). Growth figures in the results are unsourced and contradict each other (50+ companies experimenting in 2023 and a 300%+ rise in postings in 2024 in one blog; a 5,000% one-year surge in another), so no defensible count was found. Postings are now common at software firms and staffing agencies, and the title is splitting into variants: GTM Engineering and Systems Manager / Director (Ashby), GTM Systems Engineer (ConstructConnect), GTM Automation Engineer, and "GTM Engineer (Clay & Claude)". A 2026 salary market report exists (RevGuild) and eMarketer ran a 2026 FAQ on the field. Clay states that it does not offer a certification for GTM engineers.

## Capability models

No certification exists by Clay's own account; the models below are a vendor curriculum and vendor or practitioner skills lists.

1. **Clay University cohorts (AI track, GTM systems track, GTM Engineering bootcamp)**. Owner: Clay. Year: 2025–2026 (cohort pages). URL: https://university.clay.com/cohorts; https://clay.com/university/cohorts; https://cohorts.clay.com/cohorts. Grade: S.
   - AI track (4 modules, 10 days): where AI fits across the GTM stack; writing reliable AI formulas and chaining them into multi-step tasks; advanced prompting; Claygent research workflows.
   - GTM systems track (6 modules, 4 weeks): systems thinking; signal-based workflows; Clay Functions; building a total addressable market and segmentation thesis (remaining modules not shown).
   - GTM Engineering bootcamp: 12 real projects in 4 weeks, live small-group coaching, introductions to employers hiring GTM engineers.
   - Clay community answer: Clay does not offer a specific certification for GTM engineers; it points to Clay University, its GTM blog and templates (https://community.clay.com/x/content-and-events/msg_eCFCxmw2WpJL/is-there-a-certification-or-a-course-to-become-a-c).
2. **GTM Engineer skills (glossary)**. Owner: Komo (vendor). Year unknown. URL: https://komo.ai/glossary/gtm-engineer. Grade: S. Technical: API integrations, basic SQL and Python, CRM configuration, workflow automation. Revenue: ICP definition, sequencing logic, messaging architecture, signal weighting, pipeline metrics. Notes GTM engineering skews to automation and pipeline generation rather than forecasting and data governance.
3. **Practitioner skills statement**. Owner: Niv G. (LinkedIn profile/post, "Learning Clay does not make you a GTM Engineer"). Year unknown. URL: https://il.linkedin.com/in/nivge. Grade: S. Coding, SQL, data modeling, CRM internals, APIs/webhooks.
4. **The GTM Engineer Roadmap**. Owner: Bolaji Anifowose (Notion page). Year unknown. URL: https://bolajianifowose.notion.site/The-GTM-Engineer-Roadmap-475d9b4e00cb8319840081206536e486. Grade: S. Title only; contents not visible.
5. **"The Rise of the GTM Engineer: A Guide to Hiring and Workflow"**. Owner: Clay community. Year unknown. URL: https://community.clay.com/x/announcements/ptu3y4lvqkq5/the-rise-of-the-gtm-engineer-a-guide-to-hiring-and. Grade: S. Defines the role as "a technical builder who creates scalable revenue systems" (wording as relayed in the result); no competency list visible.
